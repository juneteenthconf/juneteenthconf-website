# The Manifesto — Spoken Word Script

**For: Brandi J. Brown**
**Duration: ~3 minutes**
**Direction: Start quiet, almost a whisper. Build slowly. By the end, you're not reading — you're declaring.**

---

*(Slow. Reverent. Almost a whisper.)*

They crossed an ocean in darkness.

Stolen from their homes.
Stripped of their names.
Their languages.
Their humanity.

For two hundred and forty-six years,
they were denied not just a seat at the table —
but the right to be seen as human.

They built the table.
They served the table.
They were never invited to sit.

*(Pause. Let it land.)*

Three-fifths of a person.
Counted for representation.
Excluded from it.

"No rights which the white man was bound to respect."

Centuries of being told:
"This table is not for you."

*(Shift. A glimmer of warmth enters the voice.)*

June 19th, 1865.

Galveston, Texas.
The last to hear the news.

Two and a half years after the Emancipation Proclamation,
Union soldiers arrived to announce
that enslaved people were free.

Freedom — delayed.
But not denied.

*(Stronger now. Building.)*

The chains broke.
The building began.

From freedom, they didn't just survive.
They built.

Booker T. Washington built an institution from nothing.
HBCUs became launchpads for Black excellence.

In Greenwood, Tulsa — Black commerce thrived.
Until it was burned to the ground.

They rebuilt.

Benjamin Banneker. Lewis Latimer. Granville Woods.
Madam C.J. Walker. George Washington Carver. Garrett Morgan.
Katherine Johnson.

Names they tried to erase.
Inventions they tried to steal.
Legacies they could not destroy.

*(Pause. Quiet again. This is the sacred moment.)*

Remember them.

Every name erased.
Every invention stolen.
Every life denied.

*(Beat of silence. Then the fire returns.)*

A pandemic.
A reckoning.
A refusal to wait any longer.

George Floyd. Breonna Taylor. Ahmaud Arbery. Michael Brown.

The world watched.
We proudly proclaimed that Black Lives Matter.

The tech industry posted black squares and made promises.

*(With defiance.)*

They said — "Black talent is hard to find."

*(Let it hang. Then:)*

We filled a roster of brilliant Black speakers
in sixteen days.

What traditional conferences claimed they couldn't do in a year —
we did in two weeks.

Twenty thousand people watched.
Zero permissions asked.

*(Building to the crescendo.)*

This is not a comeback.
This is an escalation.

The table was built in 2020.
In 2026, it becomes permanent.
Institutional.
A movement.

*(Full power. Declarative. No hesitation.)*

No more asking.
No more waiting.

Building.
Our.
Own.
Table.

*(Final beat. Measured. Certain.)*

We will no longer wait for acceptance.
We will no longer wait for approval.

Four hundred years — from stolen to sovereign.
From silence to stage.
From asking for a seat —
to building a table big enough for all of us.

*(Softly, an invitation.)*

Your seat is waiting.

June 19th and 20th, 2026.
Chicago.

Juneteenth Conference.

Building Our Own Table.

---

## Recording Notes

- **Tone progression:** Whisper → warmth → strength → defiance → declaration → invitation
- **Key pauses:** After "never invited to sit," after "Remember them," after "sixteen days," after "Building. Our. Own. Table."
- **The names matter:** Slow down for the inventor names and the memorial names. Let each one land.
- **"Sixteen days"** is the emotional pivot — the moment defiance becomes action. Hit it.
- **Final lines** should feel like an open hand, not a fist. The anger resolves into welcome.
- **Target length:** 2:30–3:30 depending on pacing
- **Recording format:** High quality WAV/AIFF, we'll compress for web delivery
